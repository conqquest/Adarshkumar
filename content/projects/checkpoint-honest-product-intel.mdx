**CheckPoint!!** is an independent product health intelligence platform and ultra-lightweight Android application built for **HackCBS 9.0**. It empowers Indian shoppers to scan packaged supermarket foods and feminine sanitary care products, decode back-of-pack ingredients, and receive an instant, fully explainable **A–E health and safety grade**.

- **Live Web Portal & APK Distribution**: [scanlabels.pages.dev](https://scanlabels.pages.dev/)
- **GitHub Repository**: [conqquest/checkpoint.hackcbs9.0](https://github.com/conqquest/checkpoint.hackcbs9.0)

## The Problem

Packaged foods and feminine hygiene products are staple supermarket purchases across India, yet back-of-pack labels obscure critical health risks:

- **Hidden Palm Oil** — Refined palm oil (containing ~45% saturated palmitic acid) is routinely disguised under vague terms like *"edible vegetable oil"*.
- **Cryptic Additive Codes** — Harmful chemical preservatives and emulsifiers appear as obscure INS/E-numbers (e.g., INS 503(ii), INS 500(ii)) that consumers cannot decode on the go.
- **Sanitary Pad Opaque Compositions** — Period products rarely disclose chlorine bleaching, toxic dioxin residues, synthetic fragrance compounds, or high plastic percentages.
- **Flawed AI Rating Apps** — Many modern barcode apps rely entirely on hallucinated LLM scores that produce inconsistent grades from scan to scan, lacking adherence to Indian FSSAI or BIS benchmarks.

## Core Features & Engineering Highlights

- **Deterministic FSSAI & BIS Rating Engine** — Health and safety grades are computed strictly by deterministic TypeScript algorithms, never by hallucinated LLMs. Every single point reduction is tied directly to verified nutritional benchmarks and official regulations.
- **Automated Palm Oil Penalty Engine** — Automatically detects palm olein derivatives and enforces an unavoidable 1-grade downgrade on products relying on cheap saturated palm fats.
- **Sanitary Pad Safety Lab (BIS IS:5405)** — A dedicated evaluation module auditing sanitary pads against official Indian standards, detecting chlorine-bleached wood pulp, dioxin risks, synthetic perfumes, and plastic film layers.
- **Sub-10ms Barcode Engine** — Over 880+ high-velocity Indian supermarket FMCG products pre-indexed in MongoDB Atlas for instant zero-latency scanning in grocery store aisles.
- **Gemini Vision Label OCR** — For unlisted artisanal snacks or bakery goods without registered barcodes, users can photograph the ingredient label; Google Gemini multimodal extraction parses the ingredients table into structured data before piping it into the deterministic rating engine.
- **Pregnancy & Allergen Safety Filters** — Flags pregnancy-sensitive ingredients (high-dose retinol, alcohol, unpasteurized elements, herbal stimulants) and customizable consumer allergens.
- **100% Bilingual (English & हिन्दी)** — Full native Hindi localization toggled in one tap, translating nutrition explanations, rating justifications, and warnings.
- **Ultra-Lightweight Android APK** — Built with React, Vite, and Capacitor into a high-performance Android APK (~5.7 MB) with zero ads, backed by a Neo-brutalist Cloudflare Pages promotional portal.

## Technical Architecture

| Layer | Technologies | Purpose |
|:------|:-------------|:--------|
| **Mobile App** | Capacitor 8, Android SDK | Native camera barcode scanning, flashlight control, offline scan cache |
| **Frontend** | React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS 4, Zustand | Mobile-first UI, Neo-Kinetic design system, state management |
| **Backend API** | NestJS 11, TypeScript, Express | High-throughput REST API with caching, validation pipes, and rate limiting |
| **Database** | MongoDB Atlas, Mongoose | Curated Indian FMCG product catalog and scan cache |
| **Label OCR** | Google Generative AI (Gemini Flash) | Structured multimodal extraction from back-label photographs |
| **Deployment** | Cloudflare Pages & Render | Global edge web distribution and containerized cloud API hosting |
