**TerraStorm** is a cloud-native AI platform designed for automated environmental monitoring and geospatial analytics.

## Key Highlights

- **Event-Driven Architecture** — Built using AWS serverless technologies including S3, Lambda, and SageMaker to automatically analyze satellite imagery data.
- **Scale** — Processes and tracks land-use change detection across more than 10,000 square kilometers of geographic imagery.
- **Alerts & Dashboards** — Integrated Amazon SNS for real-time automated monitoring alerts and constructed a Django-based administrative dashboard, reducing reporting generation time by 60%.
